# Critique: employer-demand draft

## VERDICT
Ready for author review as a proposed methods paper. Not ready as an empirical trend paper. No new data or findings were assessed.

## STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS
The central question and primary Check-duty measure fit the title. The source ledger separates inspiration, method provenance, and new evidence.

## METHODOLOGICAL REALITY
The cohort section separates newly observed postings from stock, and required experience from preferred experience. Board list, windows, coding-intensive families, detection of reposts, and admissible outages need to be frozen before the study. Coverage cannot be assumed from an initial title filter.

## STATISTICAL INTEGRITY
The comparison section identifies one observation per posting and a scorable-text denominator. Unknowns and matching failures are distinct. The revision makes the four-week rule a coverage gate and qualifies what model-based uncertainty could mean in a nonprobability frame. No outcomes tested.

## RESULTS & DISCUSSION
The main contribution is a feasible measurement protocol. No conclusions about hiring, work minutes, learning, or AI causation follow. Figure 1 is a workflow, not an observed trend.

## WRITING AS DESIGN
The figure presents the four gates; the companion table tells reviewers what to audit. Clarified exactly which three measures the parent study distinguishes.

## ETHICAL SCREENING
Use approved source access and release only authorized artifacts. Do not use records in contributor demos without permission; use synthetic fixtures instead.

## RANKED IMPROVEMENTS
1. [MAJOR, study] Lock date windows, source coverage rules and minimum comparable cell criteria before comparison.
2. [MAJOR, study] Pilot a versioned eligibility and deduplication protocol. Double-code a held-out set containing plausible false negatives, not just accepted candidates.
3. [MAJOR, revised] Distinguish the four-week gate from power; add common-employer attrition and within-family mix checks.
4. [MINOR, revised] Add a labeled, non-results workflow figure and cite the parent source more precisely.

## WHAT WORKS
Code duties independently of titles and qualifications. Treat missing mentorship language as missing evidence of what the employer advertises, not of what happens at work.

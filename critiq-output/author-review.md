# Silent Critique: Employer Demand

**Verdict: ready for protocol review, not an empirical trend manuscript.** The new copy keeps the main outcome at the posting level, makes it falsifiable by a fixed-composition comparison, adds a fictional bounds example, and states what must be reported before interpretation. The source manuscript and prior review are unchanged.

- **Structure:** the literature motivates a measure of assigned duties but does not deliver observed junior STEM demand.
- **Methods:** common-source windows, a fixed cohort rule, and a deliberately selected audit of rejected and missed candidates are necessary. The proposed reference-weight convention is labeled as an assumption.
- **Analysis:** changing employer composition can confound aggregate differences. Report common-support losses, missingness bounds and family contrasts, and justify any dependence model.
- **Figure and results:** the existing workflow is a design diagram; the added worked example is invented for exposition. The eventual result table needs literal numerators and both eligible and scorable denominators.
- **Ethics and reproducibility:** record rights, source outages and acquisition rules before making a reproducibility claim. Do not publish raw ads or another team's data.

Priority: lock board-day coverage and comparability before computing any trend, then freeze the weighting and error-audit plans.

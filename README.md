# The Junior’s Curve: What Employers Ask of Entry-Level STEM Workers

**Working paper and study design.** What duties, qualifications, AI-tool expectations, and support do employers advertise to entry-level STEM applicants, and how do they vary across job families and comparable periods?

Job ads measure stated demand in the sources we observe. They do not measure who was hired or how people spend their workday.
